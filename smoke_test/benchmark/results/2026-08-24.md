## Interpretation

With four workers and 0.1 seconds of simulated waiting per job, the idealized
service ceiling is approximately 40 jobs/second before Redis, Sidekiq, enqueue,
and polling overhead. This run reached 38.80 jobs/second, which is consistent
with that workload and demonstrates concurrent job progress through the real
Redis-backed Ratomic pool.

The worker uses `sleep`, so this snapshot measures I/O-style concurrency and
end-to-end throughput. It does not measure CPU-bound parallel execution or
establish a general performance claim. Repeat the benchmark on the target
hardware with a controlled workload before comparing implementation changes.
